# Adoption and follow-up plan

Pilot the job aid with a small group of coordinators. Ask them to rewrite one existing update, then compare the result against the five fields. Collect confusing wording and revise the aid before broader rollout.

## Suggested measures
Track whether the required fields are present in a defined sample of updates. Ask whether recipients can identify the decision and owner. Compare like-for-like samples before and after the pilot and state the sample size. Separate satisfaction, learning and workplace use; a positive quiz score alone does not establish behavior change.

No workplace pilot, learner study or adoption measurement has been performed for this demonstration. The interactive quiz provides feedback only within the current browser session.

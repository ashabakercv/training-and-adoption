Include five fields

  1. Outcome: what changed or was completed?
  2. Blocker: what is preventing the next milestone?
  3. Decision: what needs a choice or approval, and from whom?
  4. Action: what happens next, with an owner and date if known?
  5. Impact: what changes if the blocker remains unresolved?

Example

Account setup is complete. Duplicate enrollment records are blocking data acceptance. The client data owner needs to confirm the correction rule by 19 November. The platform administrator will re-run validation on 20 November after the decision. If it slips, the implementation lead will assess the impact on UAT.

Before sending

Check the source for each date and commitment. Preserve pending decisions. Replace an unknown owner or date with “not yet confirmed” and identify who will clarify it. Use plain language and put the decision needed near the top.
